First-in-Human (FIH) Clinical Protocol & Dose Escalation Engine

21 CFR Part 312

Translate nonclinical NHP toxicology directly into First-in-Human starting doses via CSF volume allometry, model intrathecal PK/PD distribution, and execute adaptive Bayesian Optimal Interval (BOIN) dose escalation with pre-specified DLT stopping rules.

Author: Francesco Nobile ORCID: 0009-0001-9477-3332 ↗

1. CSF Allometric Starting Dose

Computes Human Equivalent Dose (HED) and Maximum Recommended Starting Dose (MRSD).

2. Intrathecal PK/PD Simulator

Simulates 2-compartment CSF/tissue clearance and Day 90 NfL response.

3. Adaptive Escalation Engine

Applies Bayesian stopping rules and recommends escalate/retain/de-escalate.

Generate an audit-ready Phase 1 clinical protocol dossier compliant with 21 CFR Part 312 Subpart H and ICH E6(R2).
